MiCra: Crespo Productive Monitor

An interactive tool to read the productive structure, roads, areas and economic indicators of the Crespo microregion.

By Matías Martínez, economist

Initial reading

MiCra organizes productive and territorial information from the Crespo microregion into a navigable experience. The monitor lets users explore areas, roads and indicators to observe where activities are located, their scale and the logistical tensions that appear across the territory.

The tool is designed to support planning conversations among municipalities, institutions and productive actors: it turns scattered data into a shared reading of infrastructure, economic activity and intervention opportunities.
