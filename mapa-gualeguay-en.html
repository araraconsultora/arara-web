Productive map of Gualeguay

A territorial reading to locate productive activity, identify location patterns and open planning questions about Gualeguay department.

Initial reading

The productive map of Gualeguay brings together an initial georeferenced layer from Arará's Productive Observatory. Its goal is to read the territory through a practical tool: locating activity points, comparing proximity, detecting concentrations and supporting conversations about infrastructure, services, logistics and value-added opportunities.

It does not replace fieldwork or sector analysis; it works as a shared reading base for municipalities, institutions and productive actors. As the Observatory incorporates new sources, this layer can grow with industrial, commercial, agricultural and service-sector information.
